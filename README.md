# Emoji Merge Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Emoji Merge ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use our mobile game application (the "App").

## 1. Summary

Emoji Merge is designed to work without creating an account. We do not require your name, email address, phone number, or other personal identity information to play the game.

We do not collect or store your gameplay data on our servers. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to deliver and measure ads, and may personalize ads where permitted by applicable law and device settings.

## 2. Information we do not collect directly

We do not create user accounts, and we do not ask for or collect:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Contact list
- Social media accounts
- Photos, videos, or microphone/camera data
- Exact location data

## 3. Information stored on your device

The App may store the following locally on your device:

- Current board state
- Score and level progress
- Best score
- Sound and other local settings

This information is stored using the device's local storage and remains on the device unless you uninstall the App or clear app data. This information is not transmitted to us.

## 4. Advertising and third-party data collection

Emoji Merge uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob and Google may collect and process information necessary to provide, measure, and improve ad delivery, including:

- Advertising ID or similar device identifier
- Device type and operating system version
- App and ad interaction data, such as impressions and clicks
- Network information, including IP address
- General or coarse location data, where available and permitted

This data is used to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize ad content. You can manage ad personalization and reset your advertising ID from your device settings.

On iOS, the App may ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the identifier for personalized ads. On Android, you can manage ad settings in the Google Ads settings screen.

Google may act as an independent data controller for some of this information. Please see:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google Ad Settings: https://adssettings.google.com
- Google AdMob information: https://support.google.com/admob

## 5. What we do not do

We do not:

- Sell your personal information
- Build a profile of you for advertising outside the ad platform used in the App
- Run our own analytics SDK that tracks your behavior across unrelated apps or websites
- Request location access, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos

## 6. Children's privacy

Emoji Merge is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Where required by law, including COPPA in the United States, ad treatment may be limited to child-directed settings as configured by Google and applicable platform rules.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You may:

- Disable personalized ads in your device settings
- Reset the advertising ID in your device settings
- Delete App data by uninstalling or clearing local app data
- Contact us to ask questions or request data-related support

If you are in a jurisdiction that gives you privacy rights, you may be able to ask for access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data where applicable. In this App, most data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us, but you can still contact us with any privacy request.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App changes or if legal requirements change. When we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after the updated policy becomes effective means you accept the updated policy.
